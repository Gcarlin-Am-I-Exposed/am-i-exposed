Sirva Relocation Llc data breach settlement: how to claim

Sirva Relocation Llc is paying up to $4,000 per person, and the claim window is still open. Claims close December 29, 2026.

The numbers

Settlement fund$260,000
Maximum per person$4,000
What it coveredup to $4,000
Claim deadlineDecember 29, 2026
CaseRivera, et al. v. Sirva Relocation LLC, Case No. 2026CH000111, in the Circuit Court of Illinois, DuPage County

Who was covered

Anyone whose private information was potentially compromised in the September 2023 data breach.

Where to claim

File at sirvadatasettlement.com, the site run by the administrator the court appointed. That is the only place a claim counts, it is free, and nobody should ever charge you to file one.

Source: the settlement administrator

Yes. Claims are open until December 29, 2026.

How much can you get from the Sirva Relocation Llc settlement?

On the court-appointed administrator’s official site, sirvadatasettlement.com. It is the only place a claim counts, and filing is always free.
