How to read this page

What a criminal group publishes is a claim, not a confirmation. In 423 of the 449 cases there is only the attacker’s word. 26 were confirmed by the victim or verified by Have I Been Pwned. Every row states its status and where it comes from.

Country and industry are assigned by us from the name of the organisation. Where we are not confident we say "unidentified" rather than guess, so that column has gaps on purpose.

We report, we do not accuse

Every case on this page comes from what a criminal group published on its own leak site, as recorded by public trackers, from a breach verified by Have I Been Pwned, or from the press. A company appearing here means someone claimed an attack: it is not a verdict, it does not prove the company did anything wrong, and it does not prove your data is circulating.

We do not independently verify any of those claims, we do not host leaked data, and we publish none of what the groups say they hold. Every row states its status and links to its source so you can check it yourself.
